How it works

Ten business days, five checkpoints, no surprises.

01

Send the single

The master, the lyrics, the release window and any references. If there is no artwork yet, say so. We reply within 24 hours with a plan and a price.

02 · Day 1

Concept and style frames

A short call, then two or three style frames that show the look before anything moves. You approve the direction, we start.

03 · Day 5

The 30-second preview

You watch the first 30 seconds. Changes here are free and done on our time. If it is not what you wanted, you get a full refund.

04 · Day 10

The full kit lands

The video, the cuts, the Canvas, the thumbnails, the trailers and the plan arrive in one folder. Two revision rounds are included for small changes.

05 · Launch week

Run the playbook

Teasers on days one to four, trailers on days five to ten, countdown and YouTube Premiere on release day. Every post, time and caption is already written.
