The brief

A kids' rock anthem that had to work on YouTube, in the classroom and on a phone.

Divinity Roxx is a two-time Grammy nominee and was Beyoncé's bassist and musical director. For the Starchild album the character, costume and world had to be designed first, then carried into every asset. This was her tenth project with the studio.

The rollout

The three-week plan that ships with every kit.

The Launch Playbook turns the files into a schedule. This is the version the client received.

Days 1 to 4

Teasers. Two to three seconds, no title, posted to Reels, TikTok and Shorts to build curiosity.

Days 5 to 10

Trailers A to D. Ten to fifteen seconds each, split-tested to find the winning clip. Canvas uploaded.

Days 11 to 14

Countdown posts and a YouTube Premiere on Friday at 12pm EST, then live across every platform.

Days 15 to 21

Keep it going: the out-now trailers, the best vertical cuts and the caption formula from the Cheat Sheet.

Posting windows in the Playbook: Reels Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday, 9am to 12pm. TikTok Monday, Tuesday and Friday, 7 to 9am or 7 to 9pm. Read the Launch Playbook.
