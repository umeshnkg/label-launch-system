## Rules
- One accent colour: change `--accent` in `src/styles.css`, nothing else. No yellow on this site, with one exception: the hero quote highlighter (`--mark`, Umesh's call 2026-10-17).
- **Hero quotes** are a stack of three messages drawn like an inbox. Since 2026-10-20 they are out of the hero: their own section (`.testi`, "In their words") between the HBz spotlight and the case studies, with more room and type scaled by `--hq-scale`; the hero is pill, headline, lede, CTA, then the Rollout stage. (`[data-hero-quotes]` in `index.html`; data `HERO_QUOTES` in `src/data.js`; code at the top of `src/main.js`; styles `.hq*`). Quotes stay verbatim, with `<m>…</m>` around the phrase the highlighter marks. Attribution is always two lines: name (role) over the company, or name over the role ("Artist" / "Major artist"); `hl` picks the highlighted part (the company; the name when the reviewer is the company or a famous name; `role` for a credential like "2x Grammy-nominated artist"). `src: 'google'` only for reviews live on the Google profile (stars); email gets the envelope (plus `date` once verified in Gmail); Instagram gets no marker. Autoplay crossfades; a sideways swipe or ← → flings the top one. No hint text. The "10+ years with labels" card is out of the hero since 2026-10-17 (markup in git history); the logo marquee carries the labels. Fitz Madison quotes stay out.
- Copy: no em dashes, no "Kind regards", the DIY product is "Make Lyric Video", big names are "has worked with".
- Every number (views, counts, prices) must be traceable to the brief. Views come from yt-dlp, not memory.
- Only work confirmed in the Dropbox deliveries folder goes in `FEATURED` (src/releases.js) or `LYRIC_VIDEOS` (src/data.js).
- Every Spotify Canvas is shown in a `.phone` with the Spotify now-playing overlay (title, artist, progress bar, controls; no top bar, no notch). It is automatic when the video src matches a `FEATURED[].canvas`; otherwise add `data-title` / `data-artist`. Switch: `SPOTIFY_CANVAS_UI` in `src/data.js`.
- Every spotlight-style lyric video or music video (homepage spotlights, case-page headers) is a YouTube watch card: add `data-ytcard="<video id>"` to the `.yt` frame and an entry in `YT_CARDS` (`src/data.js`) with the exact YouTube title, channel and an avatar saved in `public/channels`. Not used in the release grids.
- Every vertical promo clip in a `.phone` gets the simplified Instagram Reels overlay: add the clip to `REELS` (`src/data.js`, keyed by video src).
- Every case page gets the "Built for the screen" blueprint section: a `<section data-blueprint data-reel data-canvas data-loop>` placeholder (the generator adds it from `vertical`, `canvas` and `loop` in `src/releases.js`; rendered by main.js). Set `loop: true` only after checking the last frame matches the first; set `canvasSpec: false` when the Canvas file is not 9:16. Loop stills live in `public/kit/loop/<slug>-{first,last}.webp`. Safe areas follow Meta's Reels guidance (top 14%, bottom 35%, sides 6%) and Spotify's Canvas specs (9:16, 3 to 8 s).
- Turnaround in days is shown only when it is 10 days or fewer (or has a custom `turnaroundLabel`); slower pages drop the chip and the Ordered/Delivered dates.
- Visual case study (Paradise is the pilot): optional fields in `src/releases.js` turn on extra sections. `kit` + `input` + `promise` = the hero (no YouTube card or phone there). Kit tiles are mockups by `type`: `yt` (watch card, opens the modal), `pack` with `ui: 'reels' | 'spotify'` (fanned phones, hover spreads them; needs web copies + posters of every file in the pack), `ab` (YouTube Studio "Test & compare", never invented results), `look` (boards + Approved stamp). No rollout plan or Motion Cover tiles. Four counting figures with icons sit under it; no dates in the hero (he does not want release/delivery dates shown). `look` = the "One look" section (photo + reference dots → palette → chips under every output; dot coordinates are measured, not guessed). `process` = step track (titles only, no descriptions, no days; `optional: true` steps are dashed and fold away under Rush; mood board and style frames are ONE step), `face` = eye/mouth safe-area card on a Canvas still (percent coords measured on a grid), `thumbs` = TV / desktop / phone size card, `quotes` (`kind: 'email' | 'google'`, drawn as an email / a Google review card) + `repeat` = outcome. Pack tiles hold a `<video>` per file (front autoplays, the rest play on hover). Approvals are drawn as the Approve button being clicked (`approve()` in the generator, `.ok` in case.css), never as checkboxes (in the Rollout a check mark may appear as the *result* on the board the client picked). Process steps: Brief = input icons, First cut = a few seconds of the real video (`video` + `poster`, `player: true`), Final = the bundle. Prev/next are thumbnail cards (`.casenav`). No "Just the piece you need" on case pages. Markup in `scripts/gen-releases.mjs`, styles in `src/case.css`, motion in main.js.
- Sold on their own: only the four in `SERVICES` (lyric video, visualizer, cover art, promo pack). Thumbnails, Canvases and brand guidelines come with the kit and are never priced separately.
- Every phone playing a clip in `MOTION_COVERS` (`src/data.js`) gets the Apple Music now-playing frame (`.phone--apple`); the clip is the 3:4 artwork. Motion Covers are shown on the homepage kit card, not in the Paradise kit (its two were re-exports of the Canvases).
- The whole site must carry one message (see memory `lls-core-message`): whatever the label has, from the song alone to a full concept, we develop every piece; fast, shown to them, their call or ours, rush if needed, full refund after the preview.
- **The Rollout (hero):** `src/rollout.js` is the rotation (data), `src/rollout/` the code (`derive.js` pure data to Project, `timeline.js` the 20 s cue list and runner, `render.js` the stage DOM, `index.js` the mount), `src/rollout.css` the styles, `public/kit/rollout/` the web copies. One 20-second round per project: notification (0 to 2.5 s), then real boards and the client picks or approves (to 7.5 s), then the 30-second preview with a click on the frame, a note, v2 and Approve (to 13.5 s), then the kit with platform badges and Approve (to 18 s), then clear. Add a project by appending a `ROLLOUT` entry (`{ slug }` of a `FEATURED` release, or a self-contained `record`) with its `story`; `npm test` must stay green (the entry validates: three-line notification that never wraps at 360 px, 2 to 4 real boards, files exist) and `npm run rollout:budget` under budget (project 1 at most 3.3 MB, others at most 1.5 MB). Boards come from the project's treatment or delivery; a board made for the site (`source: 'site'`) is reviewed by Umesh and saved into the project's Dropbox folder first. Previews are 4 to 6 s muted cuts of the real video (`scripts/rollout-assets.mjs preview`); boards are cut with `scripts/rollout-boards.py`. Cursor tags are exactly `Studio` and `Label / Client`, never a name. No view counts, dates or invented files in the hero; the release tag is computed from `ordered`/`released` or reads 'Date set'. One green Approve button; the only check mark is on the picked board. Platform badges are tags hanging off the bottom-right edge of a piece, never printed inside the image. `on: false` keeps a project built but out of the rotation (Rock It Out, La Cuerda and I'M FREE are off: see the comments in `src/rollout.js`). Dev view: `/?rollout=beat:<notification|directions|preview|delivered>&p=<index>`.
- **Rollout versions** (`src/rollout/version.js`, `ROLLOUT_VERSION`): both stay built; never delete version 1's code. v1 = the round described above (macOS notification, four steps). v2 (2026-10-20, live default) = beat 1 is the send: big iOS-style file icons with their names (audio first, then cover or stacked photos with a count badge), the release tag, one green Send the Label / Client cursor clicks (it reads "Sent" and the files fly off); a "+" sits between files; file names are real-looking (`story.fileNames`, e.g. `coverart.jpeg`, `artist-photo.jpeg`); input images carry `inputFocus` so faces never crop; the `from` inset on a character board is a person-framed 3:4 crop (`fromCrop`, `fromPlace: 'in'`) set beside the cartoon, with no caption and no arrow (Umesh: cleaner); nothing in v2 is hand-drawn: platform tags and the audio file use the official app icons (120 px PNGs from Wikimedia Commons in `public/kit/rollout/icons/`), glyphs are Material Symbols SVG files painted through a CSS mask (`.rl-ms`); Send vanishes when pressed (no "Sent"); every button (Send, Approve, Download) is hidden until its `reveal` cue, after its stage's graphics; Download sits above the confetti; board names sit on the bottom edge of the image; no YouTube mark on the thumbnail pack; file names read like real files with spaces ("Cover Art.jpeg", "Artist Photo.jpeg", "Event Clips.mp4"); one mock Canvas at most per project; reels that look alike are cut with `story.reelPick` (one Instagram, one TikTok); input `song+clips` = client footage, stacked text-free stills with the Material videocam mark (Bittersweet Goodbye: boards are frames of the delivered video, there was no treatment); stages 1 and 2 leave by being sucked fast into the centre of the screen (accelerating, ~0.34 s), stages 2 and 3 shoot in from the centre (~0.36 s), stage 3 fades out as in v1; every kit shows Spotify Canvases, with site-made mockups (`story.mockCanvases`, `<slug>-canvas-mock-N.webp` cut from the project's preview frames) where none were delivered (Umesh's call, 2026-10-20, overrides "no invented files" for Canvases in v2 only); the 30-second preview beat is off, so three steps in 16.5 s (`BEATS_V2`, `cuesForV2`, `renderProjectV2`, the "Version 2" block at the end of rollout.css). Revert by setting `ROLLOUT_VERSION = 1`; compare in the browser with `?rv=1` / `?rv=2`. Audio file = the iTunes vector icon on a CSS macOS document page (`AUDIO_ICON` in version.js, `?audio=` previews the others); single images (cover, artist photo) = Finder thumbnail with a white border in their own shape (`IMAGE_ICON`, `?image=`). On desktop v2's stage is 2.4:1 (`--rollout-ar` / `--rollout-k` in rollout.css; the mount restores 2:1 for v1) and the final delivery is one row (clips | YouTube card | thumbnails | Canvases, Download below); the hero is sized so pill, headline, lede, CTA and the whole stage fit a 13-inch MacBook Chrome viewport (1440 x 790): stage width = (100svh - 440px) x --rollout-k in styles.css. Picks for every hero visual live on the review artifact SkwZGypyHmMW9TBH2tMBVf (db doc review/picks). `npm run shoot:rollout` still asserts v1's beats, notification and preview (it loads pages without `?rv`), so its checks fail while v2 is live until it is updated.
- Never commit `.dev.vars`. Push only when Umesh asks (every push to `main` deploys to Cloudflare Pages).

## Commands
- `node scripts/og.mjs` (share cards + icons) · `npm run gen` (case pages) · `npm run dev` (port 5180) · `npm run build` · `npm run preview` (port 4180) · `npm run dev:full` (wrangler + Function) · `node scripts/shoot.mjs <dir>` (screenshots) · `npm test` (node --test) · `npm run shoot:rollout` (hero beats, behaviour and phone fold in a real browser; `ROLLOUT_P=<index>` for another project; modes `fallback` and `lcp`) · `npm run rollout:budget` · `node scripts/rollout-assets.mjs preview|poster|image ...` · `python -I scripts/rollout-boards.py --pdf ... | --tiles ...`

## Structure
- `index.html` landing · `releases/<slug>/index.html` case studies (8 generated from `src/releases.js` by `scripts/gen-releases.mjs`; `rock-it-out` is handwritten)
- `src/releases.js` featured case studies · `src/data.js` other lists (lyric videos, logos, services, reviews, FAQ, YT_CARDS incl. the hero's placeholder cards) · `src/rollout.js` + `src/rollout/` + `src/rollout.css` the hero · `src/overlays.js` Spotify / Reels / Apple overlay builders · `src/main.js` behaviour · `src/styles.css` tokens + layout · `tests/` node --test
- Repo: github.com/umeshnkg/label-launch-system -> Cloudflare Pages (same method as ughdstudios.com)
- `functions/api/lead.js` lead form -> ClickUp (custom fields matched by name) + Resend (team copy + confirmation to the lead) + hot/warm/cold lead score; field decisions are in the vault's `lead-form-intake-synthesis.md`, do not add form fields without reading it
- `public/thumbs` YouTube thumbs · `public/kit` client kit images + Canvas loops · `public/docs` PDFs · `public/kit/rollout` the hero's boards, previews, posters and `fallback.webp` (no-JS image)
- Share previews: every page carries og:* + twitter:* tags with a 1200x630 JPG card from `public/og/` (homepage `home.jpg`, case pages `<slug>.jpg`). Cards are rendered by `node scripts/og.mjs` (add `--concepts` for all homepage concepts; the live one is `HOME` in that script). Re-run it after changing a case's title, views or poster. Never point og:image at a .webp or a video frame.
